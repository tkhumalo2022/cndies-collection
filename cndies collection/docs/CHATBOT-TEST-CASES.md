# CNDIE'S Assistant acceptance checks

Use these checks after deployment:

1. Open the assistant and verify the launcher hides while the panel is open.
2. Choose **Find me an iPhone** > **Under R5 000** > **Best from both** and confirm product recommendations appear.
3. Type **iPhone 13** and confirm current matching catalogue items appear.
4. Type **budget R8000** and confirm the assistant asks for condition preference.
5. Choose **View in shop** and confirm the storefront search filters to that phone.
6. Choose **Delivery**, **Payment**, and **Warranty & quality** and confirm guidance appears.
7. Choose a WhatsApp action and confirm it opens the existing store WhatsApp number.
8. Press Escape and confirm the assistant closes.
9. On mobile, confirm the assistant behaves as a bottom sheet and does not overlap the WhatsApp shortcut when open.
10. With reduced-motion enabled, confirm reveal/chat transitions are minimized.
