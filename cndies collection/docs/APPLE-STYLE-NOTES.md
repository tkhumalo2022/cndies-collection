# CNDIE'S Collection storefront interaction principles

This storefront borrows interaction principles commonly used in polished Apple-platform experiences without copying Apple branding or product-page content.

- Clear visual hierarchy and product-first presentation.
- Large, familiar controls with comfortable touch targets.
- Translucent materials used to communicate depth and grouping.
- Progressive disclosure in the sales assistant instead of overwhelming users with choices.
- Calm, short motion with a reduced-motion fallback.
- Keyboard focus states and descriptive labels for accessibility.
- A direct path from product discovery to WhatsApp confirmation.

The chatbot is intentionally local and deterministic. It reads the storefront catalogue already loaded in the browser, recommends matching phones, filters the shop, and escalates to WhatsApp for stock, delivery, warranty and payment confirmation. No API key or external AI service is required.
